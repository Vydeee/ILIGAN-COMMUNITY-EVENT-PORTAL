About the Portal

Connecting Iliganons Through Culture, Heritage & Community

Our Civic Mission

Empowering All 44 Barangays of the City of Majestic Waterfalls

Iligan City is home to a rich tapestry of Maranao, Higaonon, and Visayan traditions—celebrated annually during the month-long Kasadya sa Diyandi Festival in honor of Senior San Miguel, alongside year-round eco-tourism runs, culinary expos, and barangay assemblies.

The Iligan Community Event Portal was built to bridge citizens, tourists, and local event coordinators in one accessible web platform. Whether you are reserving a free seat for a cultural night at Anahaw Amphitheater or organizing a coastal clean-up in Barangay Dalipuga, our platform streamlines discovery and attendance.

      Booking and passes

      How do I reserve a pass?

      Open Discover Events, choose an available event, and select Book Event Pass. Check your name, contact details, and ticket quantity before confirming. Your reservation is saved in My Bookings and Passes.

      Can I cancel or restore a booking?

      Select Cancel Booking in My Bookings and confirm your choice. Seats are released immediately. Use Undo within five seconds, or Restore Booking later if seats are still available.

      Does a paid listing collect payment here?

      No online payment is collected by this student project. The displayed amount describes the reservation. Confirm payment arrangements with the event organizer.

      Accounts and roles

      Which account should I choose?

      Citizens discover events and manage their own passes. Coordinators publish and manage their own events. Administrators oversee citywide listings, portal content, locations, and support inquiries.

      Why do coordinators need a registration key?

      The coordinator key limits event-publishing accounts to approved users. Citizens do not need a key. Ask the project administrator for coordinator access.

      Publishing events

      What should I prepare before publishing?

      Prepare an event title, description, category, barangay, venue, date, time, price, seating capacity, and image. Duplicate titles trigger a warning. Coordinators can edit their own listings and view their attendees.

      What happens when an event is deleted?

      Event deletion is permanent and removes its related bookings. The confirmation dialog requires the word DELETE. Cancel the dialog if you are unsure.

      Privacy and support

      How is my information used?

      Your account and reservation details are stored in the project database. Authorized coordinators can view attendees for their own events. Administrators can oversee listings and support inquiries.

      Need help with an account or reservation? Send a support inquiry.

      Privacy notice

      This student project stores account names, email addresses, phone numbers, reservations, and support inquiries in its MySQL database.

      Citizens see their own bookings. Coordinators see attendee details for the events they manage. Administrators oversee listings and submitted support inquiries. Do not submit sensitive information in support messages.

      Contact inquiries are saved in the portal inbox. The project does not currently send email replies automatically or collect online payments.

      For a correction or deletion request, use Contact Us and include the relevant booking reference. Sample listings are demonstration content.
